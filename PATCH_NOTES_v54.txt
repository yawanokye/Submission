UCC-CoDE eServices v54
Project Work submission gate and warning policy

SUBMISSION IS ACCEPTED WHEN

1. The number of groups stated in the portal claim field is a valid positive number.
2. That number equals the number of completed project-work files attached.
3. Every registration or index number found in the score sheet has a score.
4. The required claim form, supervisor report, score sheet and completed project-work files are attached.
5. The required claimant details, declaration, department and student category are supplied.

WHAT NO LONGER BLOCKS SUBMISSION

The following variations are saved as warnings for departmental review rather than rejecting the submission:

- score-sheet group count differs from the number claimed;
- centre codes differ within a group;
- the programme, centre or group combination is unclear;
- student name, index number or group number is missing on an extracted row;
- S/N entries are out of sequence;
- a score is non-numeric or outside 0–100;
- an index number is repeated within the score sheet;
- the supervisor, centre or index number appears in an earlier submission;
- a centre pattern needs administrator reconciliation.

WHAT STILL BLOCKS SUBMISSION

- claimed group total and attached completed project-work count do not match;
- a registration or index number has no score;
- no registration or index number with a score is present;
- a required file or required claimant field is missing;
- the score sheet does not contain the five required headings;
- an invalid department, category, centre selection, email address or claimant declaration is supplied.

USER AND ADMIN DISPLAY

- The score-sheet check now confirms that every index number has a score.
- The successful submission message states how many variations were recorded as warnings.
- The departmental portal continues to display each warning under Verification Warnings.
- Scores still enter consolidated and master reports only after departmental approval.
- The v53 reporting-centre, transfer reconciliation, audit history and immutable processing-batch rules remain active.
