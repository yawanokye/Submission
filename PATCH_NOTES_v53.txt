UCC-CoDE eServices v53
Project Work predominant-centre grouping and transfer reconciliation

WHAT CHANGED

1. Project Work groups are still identified by Programme Code + Centre + Group Number.
2. The programme code is read from the first registration/index-number segment.
3. The original registration/index number and its centre code are never rewritten by this rule.
4. If the same programme and group number contains a unique predominant centre with at least three students, no more than two minority-centre students, and a final combined size of three to six, the minority students remain with that group under the predominant reporting centre.
5. If two centre clusters both contain normal groups of three to six students, they remain separate groups even when their group numbers are the same.
6. Tied, unclear, unclassified, or oversized centre patterns are shown to the Project Work administrator and cannot be approved until an administrator records a decision.
7. Administrators can confirm that centre groups are separate and must provide a reason/evidence. A safe automatic predominant-centre result can also be overridden and kept separate.
8. Consolidated, master, centre-by-centre, processing-batch and payment-unit outputs use the reporting centre. The uploaded score workbook remains unchanged.
9. Every manual centre decision is recorded in the claim audit history. Automatic predominant-centre applications are also visible in the audit history.
10. A centre decision made after approval returns the submission to Pending Verification, invalidates later payment approval where applicable, increments its processing version, and adds correction rows to affected immutable processing batches.

RULE EXAMPLES

- Programme BEP, Group 1: AS/01 has 4 students and AS/02 has 2 students. The six students are one group reported under AS/01; both AS/02 index numbers remain unchanged.
- Programme BEP, Group 1: AS/01 has 3 students and AS/02 has 3 students. These are two distinct centre groups.
- Programme BEP, Group 1: AS/01 has 2 students and AS/02 has 2 students. Approval is blocked until the administrator confirms the centre treatment.
- Programme BEP, Group 1: AS/01 has 6 students and AS/02 has 1 student. The system does not merge them because the final group would exceed six; administrator review is required.
