UCC-CoDE eServices v56
Administrator reconciliation decisions and numeric group output

1. Suggested administrator resolutions

The Group / Claim Reconciliation panel now distinguishes likely transferred-student cases from registration/index formatting mistakes. It recommends the safest available action while retaining the existing manual alternatives.

Examples:

- A three-student group with two ER/04 records and one BA/01 record is suggested for reporting under ER/04 as a transferred-student case.
- BCH/ER04/22/0022 is suggested as BCH/ER/04/22/0022 when the corrected centre code matches the other students in the same programme and group.

2. Comments and sequential decisions

Every accepted suggestion or manual centre decision requires an administrator comment. A decision is saved even when another case in the same submission is still waiting. This corrects the earlier behaviour where the first decision could fail because a second unresolved case remained.

The portal shows the saved decision comments, administrator and date. The full history is also available in the claim audit.

3. Warning clearance and downstream validity

After every decision, the system recalculates the claim, reporting-centre groups and attached project works. When they agree:

- the reconciliation warning is removed;
- the panel turns to the resolved state;
- the record is marked valid for further processing;
- consolidation approval, processing batches, claims and later payment stages use the resolved data.

The ordinary consolidation approval, claimant certification and HoD approval controls remain in force.

4. Group number output

Project Work consolidated, master, programme-by-centre and centre-by-centre outputs now write only the numeric group identifier in GROUP NO.

- Group1 becomes 1.
- Group 1 becomes 1.
- Procurement 1 becomes 1.
- HRM G1 becomes 1.

The original group text remains unchanged in the uploaded source workbook.

5. Verification

The v56 automated test covers a submission with eight claimed groups, eight completed works, a 2-to-1 transferred-student centre pattern and a malformed centre code. It verifies partial decision persistence, suggested correction, warning clearance, downstream payment units and numeric group values in the exported consolidated workbook.
