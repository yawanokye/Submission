UCC-CoDE eServices v61
Unique Consolidated Claims and Duplicate-Payment Protection

HoD consolidated register

1. Multiple unique approved submissions from the same claimant are grouped under one consolidated claim number.
2. The grouping key uses the department, calendar year, student category and claimant identity.
3. Distance and Non-Residential claims never share a consolidated claim number.
4. The department portal displays separate Distance and Non-Residential consolidated registers.
5. The downloadable workbook contains separate HoD Distance Claims and HoD Non-Residential worksheets, plus source-submission details.

Duplicate-payment reconciliation

1. Project Work comparison uses the approved student registration or index number, group and score.
2. Field Experience and Teaching Practice comparison uses the approved student registration or index number, assessment category and score values.
3. If the same student results are submitted twice, the earliest valid approved submission remains payable.
4. A later exact repeat is labelled Duplicate student results and cannot be forwarded to the HoD.
5. If the same students are submitted again with different scores, the later submission is labelled as a conflicting repeat and is also blocked.
6. For Project Work and Field Experience, the comparison works across claimants to prevent the same student results from being claimed by another person.
7. Assessor and vetting claims remain claimant-specific because the same dissertation may legitimately be assigned to more than one assessor.
8. HoD payment approval repeats the duplicate check. A claim that was approved for consolidation before this update cannot bypass the control.

Audit and source protection

The original submissions remain unchanged. The consolidated register lists every included source reference. Duplicate submissions remain visible with the earlier payable reference and a clear reason for exclusion.

Verification

- JavaScript syntax and DOM-contract checks pass.
- The claim-routing test verifies two unique Distance submissions consolidate under one number.
- It verifies the same claimant receives a separate Non-Residential consolidated number.
- It verifies an exact repeat of the same student results is excluded from totals, blocked from HoD forwarding and rejected at HoD payment approval.
- Existing claim-routing and payment-workflow tests continue to pass.
