UCC-CoDE eServices v60
HoD or Assigned Administrator Certification-Waiver Confirmation

What changed

1. A claimant-certification waiver may now be confirmed by either:
   - an individual authorised HoD for the department; or
   - an individual departmental administrator assigned to the claim's functional section.

2. The confirming person must enter their own account password. Shared department master credentials and Developer Preview cannot confirm a waiver.

3. The permission applies to both single-claim and bulk certification waivers. For a bulk action, the account must be authorised for every selected claim.

4. Each waiver records the confirming officer's name, email address and authority role, together with the selected reasons, evidence source, optional details, date and time.

5. The authority role appears in department views, downstream functional-unit views, Payroll views, approved claim PDFs and downloadable registers.

Important authority boundary

This change does not delegate HoD payment approval. Uploading the protected HoD signature and approving a claim for payment remain restricted to an individual account configured as the authorised HoD for that department.

Verification

- JavaScript syntax and DOM-contract checks are included in npm run check.
- The claim-routing test verifies that an assigned departmental administrator can complete a bulk certification waiver.
- The same test verifies that the assigned administrator is still rejected from the HoD payment-approval action.
- Existing claim-routing and payment-workflow tests verify the remaining approval chain.
