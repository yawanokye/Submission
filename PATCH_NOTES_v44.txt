CoDE Academic Services v44.0.0
Duplicate score policy and audited administrator removal

IMPLEMENTED

1. Exact duplicate rule
   - Student names are compared without case, punctuation or spacing differences.
   - Registration or index numbers are compared without case or spacing differences.
   - Numeric score formats such as 80 and 80.0 are treated as the same score.
   - For multi-score Field Experience records, all score fields must match.
   - Exact matches are accepted and counted once in approved consolidated and master outputs.

2. Conflicting duplicate rule
   - A matching registration or index number with a different name or score remains a warning.
   - The administrator can correct the registration or index number in the existing reconciliation panel.
   - The administrator can remove a selected conflicting row from approved records and provide a reason.

3. Preservation and audit
   - Row removal does not alter or delete the original uploaded workbook.
   - The audit trail records the submission, row number, student, registration or index number, score, reason, administrator and date.
   - A score sheet cannot be left with zero approved rows through row removal. The administrator must keep at least one row or delete the whole submission separately.
   - Any approved payment document is invalidated when the approved score data changes.

4. Portal display
   - Green status identifies an exact duplicate that will be counted once.
   - Red status identifies a conflict requiring correction or removal.
   - The source workbook, extracted sheet and whole-submission delete action remain available.
