Academic Submission Portals

Select the appropriate public portal. Each form also asks you to select the department that should receive the submission.

1

Undergraduate Project Work

For supervisors/examiners submitting project work records and scores.

  • Department selection
  • Claim form and report
  • Excel score sheet validation
  • Approved-only score consolidation
Open Project Work Portal
2

Field Experience Scores

For supervisors/examiners submitting Field Experience score sheets.

  • Department selection
  • Study centre selection
  • Excel score sheet validation
  • Pending departmental verification
Open Field Experience Portal
3

Dissertation Submission

For students submitting fresh, revised or final dissertations.

  • Fresh dissertation
  • Revised dissertation + reviewer responses
  • Final dissertation + responses + Turnitin report
  • Department routing
Open Dissertation Portal
4

Assessment Report Submission

For assessors returning assessment documentation.

  • Department selection
  • Number of works being submitted
  • Assessment or vetting reports
  • Claim forms and score sheets
  • Optional reviewed dissertations
Open Assessment Portal
Each successful submission generates a unique reference number and is visible only to the administrator of the selected department.
