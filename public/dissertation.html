Dissertation

Capitalisation, punctuation and line breaks are ignored during matching, but the wording must match the uploaded work.
Accepted formats: searchable PDF, DOC and DOCX. The server reads the beginning of the uploaded work and validates the entered title before saving the submission.
Title validation occurs during submission. If the title cannot be found in the uploaded work, the submission is rejected and the uploaded file is removed.
A unique submission reference will be generated only after the title is successfully validated.
