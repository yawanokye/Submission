UCC-CoDE eServices v55
Centre/group reconciliation warning correction

CORRECTED REQUIREMENT

The non-blocking warning applies specifically to Project Work centre and group reconciliation.

Example now accepted:

- groups stated in the claim/portal: 10;
- completed project works attached: 10;
- programme-centre-group combinations detected in the score sheet: 11;
- every registration or index number has a score.

The submission is saved as Pending Verification. The difference between 10 claimed groups and 11 detected programme-centre-group combinations is displayed to the departmental administrator as a reconciliation warning. It no longer rejects the submission.

SUBMISSION BLOCKERS

- groups stated in the claim/portal do not equal completed project works attached;
- a registration or index number has no score;
- no registration or index number with a score is present;
- the required claim form, report, score sheet or completed project work is missing;
- required claimant, department, category, centre-selection, email or declaration information is invalid;
- the score sheet lacks one or more of the five required headings.

SCOPE CORRECTION FROM v54

v55 narrows the new warning rule to centre/group reconciliation. It removes the additional row-quality warning classifications introduced in v54 for missing names, missing group numbers, S/N variations, duplicate indexes within one sheet and unusual score values. Those matters retain the system behaviour that existed before v54.

USER DISPLAY

After a successful submission with a centre/group difference, the claimant sees that the reconciliation was recorded as a warning and the submission was accepted. The departmental portal shows the detailed group-reconciliation issue for review.
