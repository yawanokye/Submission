UCC-CoDE eServices v46.0.0
============================

Branding update
---------------
- Replaced the former public-facing service name with “UCC-CoDE eServices”.
- Renamed the previous portal title to “CoDE eServices Portal”.
- Updated page titles, headers, footers, emails, health status, deployment examples and PDF metadata.

Logo and browser identity
-------------------------
- Replaced the header logo with the supplied University of Cape Coast crest.
- Added a multi-size favicon for browser tabs and URLs.
- Added an Apple touch icon for iPhone and iPad bookmarks and home-screen shortcuts.
- Added explicit icon links to every public, administrative, developer, payroll and auditor page.

Deployment safety
-----------------
- Preserved the existing Render service name and persistent-disk mount path so the live service remains attached to its current stored submissions, complaints, accounts and resources.
